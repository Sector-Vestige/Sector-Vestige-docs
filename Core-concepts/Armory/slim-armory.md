# Slim out the armory
More viarity in what the GM's can give the crew and limiting the crew to get new longarms.
Plus making a NanoTrasen lore accurate armory. (Energy based weaponry)

## Problem:
The crew currently has a platoonsworth of weapons sitting in the armory making them overprepared against everything.
It has been proven that when the crew has less resources the GM's can make more fun events because then they're more dependent on the GM's

## Solution:
- Slim out the armory first with removing most of the ballistic weaponry under the pretence of ECO & Sustainibility. (idk something cheesy)
- Introduce a "Grant" system where the crew gets a LEGAL grant from central command to buy bigger weapons per GM discretion.
- Completely overhaul the weapons but another design doc needs to be written up for that.

Summary:
Gut the current armory. Change how the crew gets their weapons in a way that gives the gm’s more control. Make all relevant factions have weapon standards for adding new weapons.

Station Weapon License Tiers
Much like the tiers of escalation in sec SOP, lethal means have “tiers” of force. These tiers practically fall under Civilian, Paramilitary, and Military.
	Civilian Tier Weapons are the average person could reasonably have without much issue. Usage ranges from hunting to personal defense. This generally encompasses most Metal Blades, QOL Implants/Cybernetics, Standard Energy Weapons, Handguns, Shotguns, and Non-Automatic Rifles. Ammo for this tier consists of purely rubbers, standard ballistics, gauss darts, standard energy emissions. 
	Paramilitary Tier Weapons are one that a private security company would use on a day to day. Usage ranges to clearing out hostile fauna to securing a high value area. This generally encompasses Energy Blades, Combat Implants/Cybernetics, High Energy Weapons, Semi-Automatic Rifles, EMP Weaponry, Low Mass Gauss, and Breaching Explosives.
Military Tier Weapons are ones that are primarily used between two warring factions, and are designed around lethality and/or stealth. Usage ranges from raiding an enemy warship to assassinations. This generally encompasses Exotic Energy Weapons, Radiation Weapons, Fully Automatic Firearms, High Mass Gauss, Gas Weaponry, Lethal Explosives, and anything not covered by the previous tiers. Ammo for this tier consists of Uranium, Incendiary, AP Rounds. 

Armory Content Changes
Most of the current armory will be gutted and redistributed across various factions.The crew will start mostly with a sparse collection of Civilian Tier energy weapons. The security lathe will be replaced with a security circuit printer + ammo fab. Cargo will no longer be able to order non NT-Crew Civilian Tier Weapons round start. Security science research will be replaced with various weapon augments or utility items.
To get any Paramilitary tier weapons by themselves, the crew will be required to acquire a temporary weapons license for the station. This license can be used to allow cargo to order stronger guns. Licenses are to generally be acquired from CC for a fee with a reasoning. The exact permissions of the licenses can be split up between general use cases, such as EMP + Breaching for Anti-AI combat. Both the price of the weapons and the licenses should put a hole in Cargo’s pocket.
For Military tier weapons, they will need to be found or directly supplied by CC. Admin intervention will be required for their use and thus they should be saved for special occasions. 

New Weapon Standards:
KEW stands for Kinetic Energy Weapon. Conventional Projectile, Gauss, and Rocket, all fall under this label. 
DEW stands for Direct Energy Weapon. Lasers, Plasma, Flame Throwers, Arc, and EMP’s all fall under this Label.
KEWs should try not to add custom ammo types for one specific weapon.
DEWs should strive to sound impactful. Less beam attack more steam explosion.
DEWs should be named in the following convention:
	DEW-[conventional power + suffix (i for internal, e for wired energy)] + Name
Ex. DEW-50e Tempest
All Weapons should strive to be unique, especially among all their faction weapons. 
Weapons should generally fall within their faction’s theme. (Ex. RND’s Arc Weaponry)

Energy Weapon Reworks
A majority of existing energy weapons will be shelved in favor of Vestige’s own take on energy weapons, being the aforementioned DEWs. All DEWs (Minus Flamethrowers) will be charged by a method of direct electrical input. The most common of these will be powerpacks, wearable equipment designed to connect directly to the weapon and act as its power source. DEWs will be unable to hold their own charge unless they have an internal power cell. Larger wearable powerpacks will be able to have a weapon clipped to them depending on their size. Powerpacks will require a do-after to equip. Larger weapons should be able to be directly jacked into an external power source, such as a generator or SMES. Lasers will no longer make pew pew sounds. 

  Lasers
A laser should be a hitscan beam that makes shockwaves and burning noises. Depending on their power, they should be Blue-Green for lower power, Translucent Blue-Purple (Ultraviolet) for medium power, and invisible besides visible distortion (Gamma Ray) for high power.
	Plasma
Plasma weapons, while technically being DEWs, are a mix of high energy and kinetic projectiles. They shoot a bolt of extreme temperature hydrogen at a target. They should require both a gas tank and power connection to function. They should be treated as very expensive and not used by a wider public. Plasma projectiles should be a ghostly purple in color. Plasma projectiles should do structural damage. 
	Arc
Arc weapons are experimental weapons that channel large electric currents through and atmosphere into a target. They achieve this by either firing a small particle that harnesses electrical anomalous energy to fire a “lightning ball” or by shooting an “anomalous grounding anchor” that creates a path of least resistance for the energy to travel down into the target.
	Emp
Emp weapons should have either a cone or sphere of influence that disrupts electronic devices using the existing emp function.
